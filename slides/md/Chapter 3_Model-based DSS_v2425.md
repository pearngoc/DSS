## Slide 4 — Model-Driven DSS

- A type of decision support system that primarily uses mathematical, statistical, and simulation models to process data and support decision-making.
- Focus on analyzing complex scenarios, testing hypotheses, forecasting outcomes, and suggesting optimal solutions based on input data.
- **Modeling is a key element**.
  - **Quantitative Models**
  - Qualitative Models

How a decision model combines choices (decision variables), uncertainties (uncontrollable variables), and formulas (mathematical relationships) to produce outcomes (result variables) for decision-making?

## Slide 13 — Data Mining Concept

- A process for **extracting hidden patterns**, which cannot be observed easily by human, from **large amounts** of data (data warehouse, big data).
  - Also named as **knowledge mining** or **knowledge discovery**
- Application:
  - Customer Segmentation in Commerce
  - Fraud Transaction Detection in Banking and Finance
  - Inventory Optimization in Retailing and Logistics
  - Other Applications: Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.

## Slide 15 — Data Mining Process

- Guideline for building a data mining project
- Most common processes:
  - **CRISP-DM** (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)
  - **SEMMA** (Sample, Explore, Modify, Model, and Assess)
  - **KDD** (Knowledge Discovery in Databases)

## Slide 16 — Data Mining Process (2)

- Proposed by European Consortium in 1990s
- Consists of 6 consecutive steps:
  - Step 1: Business Understanding

Step 2: Data Understanding

  - Step 3: Data Preparation
  - Step 4: Model Building
  - Step 5: Testing & Evaluation
  - Step 6: Deployment

Accounts for ~85% of total project time

## Slide 18 — Data Mining Tasks (2)

- **Prediction (Forecasting)**
  - Classification: Assigns data into predefined categories.
  - Regression: Predicts continuous values.
  - Time series: Predicts future values based on past data.
- Algorithms
  - Decision Trees, Neural Networks, SVM, Naïve Bayes, etc.
- Learning Types: Supervised Learning

## Slide 19 — Data Mining Tasks (3)

- **Association**
  - Market-Basket: Identifies frequently co-occurring items.
  - Link Analysis: Finds relationships between data points.
  - Sequence Analysis: Identifies patterns over time.
- Algorithms
  - Apriori, OneZ,...
- Learning Types: Unsupervised Learning

## Slide 20 — Data Mining Tasks (4)

- **Segmentation**
  - Clustering: Groups data into clusters based on similarity.
  - Outlier Analysis: Identifies unusual data points.
- Algorithms
  - K-means, Expectation Maximization.
- Learning Type: Unsupervised Learning
